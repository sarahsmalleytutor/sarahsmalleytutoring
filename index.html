My approach

I am an experienced mathematics and physics teacher, examiner and tutor with a degree in Engineering from the University of Oxford (St Anne's College). I teach at a leading, academically selective, independent day and boarding school in south-east London.

Every student is different. My aim is to understand how each pupil thinks and learns, identify the areas where they need support, and then build their understanding and confidence step by step. I combine subject expertise with a calm, patient and encouraging approach. I want students to feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes and tackling challenging material without feeling overwhelmed. As an experienced mathematics and physics teacher, examiner and tutor, I have a clear understanding of both the demands of the curriculum and what is required to perform well in examinations.

I also have extensive experience supporting pupils with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), and I adapt my teaching to the individual student wherever appropriate.
